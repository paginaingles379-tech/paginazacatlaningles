Visit Zacatlán 🍎

Culture, Nature & Apples

History 🍎

Zacatlán was founded in 1562 during the Spanish colonial period and has deep pre-Hispanic roots linked to Nahua communities. These indigenous groups settled in the region due to its fertile land, abundant water, and cool mountain climate.

Its name comes from the Nahuatl word “Zacatlán”, meaning “place of grass”, which reflects the natural environment of the area. During colonial times, agriculture became the main economic activity, shaping the town’s development and daily life.

Over the years, Zacatlán became internationally known for its apple farming, earning recognition as one of the most important apple-producing regions in Mexico. Another defining element of its identity is clock manufacturing, an industry that brought craftsmanship, innovation, and worldwide prestige to the town. Today, apples and clocks symbolize Zacatlán’s tradition and history.

Culture 🍎

Zacatlán preserves indigenous traditions mixed with Spanish colonial heritage. Its churches, streets, and public squares reflect colonial architecture, while local customs remain strong in everyday life.

Traditional music, dances, artisan crafts, and religious celebrations are deeply rooted in the community. The people of Zacatlán are known for their hospitality, strong family values, and community pride, making visitors feel welcomed.

Traditions 🍎

The Apple Fair, celebrated every August, is the most important festivity in Zacatlán. This celebration honors the apple harvest and the town’s agricultural heritage.

The festival includes traditional dances, colorful parades, live music, cider tasting, handcrafted products, cultural exhibitions, and religious events. Throughout the year, other local festivities preserve ancestral customs and strengthen community bonds.

Food 🍎

Zacatlán is famous for its apple cider, apple bread, jams, and traditional sweets made using family recipes passed down through generations.

Local cuisine also includes tamales, mole poblano, pastries, homemade desserts, and regional dishes prepared with fresh local ingredients, offering authentic flavors to visitors.

Why Visit Zacatlán? 🍎

Zacatlán offers fresh mountain air, foggy landscapes, waterfalls, rich culture, and delicious traditional food. Its natural beauty creates a peaceful and magical atmosphere.

With viewpoints above the clouds, cultural festivals, historic streets, and warm hospitality, Zacatlán is an ideal destination for relaxation, photography, adventure, and cultural tourism.
